Seven design options for your new About Us page

We studied how larger staffing companies introduce themselves, then built seven different versions of your About Us page using your own content. Please review them and tell us which one you prefer.

Please note: this page is a presentation for you to review. It is not your About Us page. The About Us pages are the seven options below, and each opens in a new tab.

The seven options

Options 1 to 3 are our newest directions, inspired by a second round of research (Michael Page, Quess, Randstad India, Kelly). Option 4 uses the exact colours of your logo. Options 5 to 7 are the earlier directions. The core content is the same in all seven.

Option 1 of 7 · Statement
easyrecruitz.com/about-us  (proposed)

Bold, colourful, confident.

Why we designed it this way

Michael Page puts a floating “Ready to hire?” card over its hero, and Kelly pairs heavy type with one italic word. Here, both of your audiences meet an action card in the first screen, and solid colour blocks make the page easy to scan.

Choose this if you want to stand out and look modern, but still approachable.

Option 2 of 7 · Arch (dark)
easyrecruitz.com/about-us  (proposed)

Dark, techy, high-energy.

Why we designed it this way

Quess uses a dark hero, a big blue curve and a persistent scam alert. Here, people rise out of an arch, services read like a menu, and a scam-alert chip follows the visitor, which is a real concern for job seekers in India.

Choose this if you want to look like a digital-first, technology-led staffing firm.

What all seven options share (and why)

  • One clear promise at the top. Visitors should know in five seconds what you do.
  • Your numbers right below it. Proof first, before the visitor scrolls.
  • Two audiences, two journeys. Companies that hire and people who want a job each get their own path.
  • One main action. "Talk to a recruiter" replaces four competing buttons.
  • Real people, not clutter. Photos and icons are placed deliberately next to short text.
  • Works on a phone. Layouts adapt to any screen size.
  • More than your current page says. Team profiles, industries, a day-by-day hiring timeline, client stories, a "where to start" picker and a safety notice. Your tagline and motto are unchanged. The new content is sample content, marked on the page.
