Who we are

A recruitment partner for people who are too busy to chase resumes.

We are a recruitment and staffing firm based in Jasola, New Delhi. We have been hiring for clients for over two years, mostly startups and growing companies that need good people quickly and can't afford a wrong hire.

Our recruiters take time to understand the business before they call a single candidate. That is the difference between a stack of resumes and a shortlist.

What we do

  • Permanent hiring

    Full-time roles at every level, from first hires to team leads.

  • Mass recruitment

    Hiring drives for dozens or hundreds of people, planned and run to a deadline.

  • Temporary staffing

    Short-term and seasonal manpower through our Temporary Staffing Division.

  • Verification and assessment

    Background checks and skill assessments before a profile reaches you.

How it works

Hiring or job hunting, here is what to expect.

Two different journeys, one team behind both. Pick yours.

  1. Tell us what you need You

    Share the role, the team and the timeline in a short call or form.

  2. We search and screen Us

    We source candidates and interview them against your brief.

  3. We verify Us

    Documents, background and skills are checked before you see a profile.

  4. Interview and hire You

    You meet the shortlist. We support the offer and the joining.
