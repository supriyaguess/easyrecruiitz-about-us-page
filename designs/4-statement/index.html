Who we are

Easy Recruitz 365 is a talent partner for companies that are growing faster than their HR team. We handle sourcing, screening, verification and joining follow-up, so hiring managers can stay on their actual jobs.

Whether it is one important hire or a hundred people next month, the same team stays with the requirement from the first call to the first day.

Permanent hiring

Full-time roles at every level, from a first hire to a team lead.

Mass recruitment

Temporary staffing

Background checks and skill assessments before a profile reaches you.

Stay safe

How we will, and will not, contact youTo be confirmed

  • We never charge job seekers

    Finding you a job is free. Nobody from our team will ask for a fee, deposit or “registration charge”.

  • We write only from our official email

    Messages come from @easyrecruitz.com. Treat any other address, including free email accounts, as suspicious.

  • We do not hire over chat alone

    Every candidate speaks to a recruiter and to the employer. We never offer a job through a messaging app only.
