Who we are

Easy Recruitz 365 is a talent partner for companies that are growing faster than their HR team. We handle sourcing, screening, verification and joining follow-up, so hiring managers can stay on their actual jobs.

Whether it is one important hire or a hundred people next month, the same team stays with the requirement from the first call to the first day.

What we do

Permanent hiring

Full-time roles across levels and functions, from first hires to team leads.

Mass recruitment

Large hiring drives planned around your start dates and headcount.

Temporary staffing

Short-term and seasonal manpower through our Temporary Staffing Division.

Verification and assessment

Background verification and skill assessments, done before profiles are shared.
