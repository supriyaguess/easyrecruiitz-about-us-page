About Easy Recruitz 365

Hiring, done by people who care how it turns out.

Easy Recruitz 365 is a recruitment and staffing firm in Jasola, New Delhi. For over two years we have worked with startups and growing companies that needed good people quickly, and could not afford the wrong ones. This page is a short introduction to how we work.

Hiring? See how we work with employers. Looking for a job? See how we work with candidates.

What we believe

Speed should not cost you quality. A quick hire is only useful if the person is right. Our shortlists are curated for your needs, not padded to look impressive.

People come first. That means candidates as much as clients. We care about experience, cultural fit and whether someone will still be happy in the job a year later.

Recruiters should understand your business. We aim to work as talent strategists, not as one more HR person you have to brief from scratch.

Hiring has to keep up with how people work now. Hybrid, remote or on-site, we find people who fit how you actually operate.
